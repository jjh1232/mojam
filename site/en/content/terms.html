What the service is

A free tool that turns text garbled by an encoding mismatch back into the original. There is no sign-up and no user data is retained. All processing happens inside your browser.

Results are not guaranteed

This tool infers the most plausible answer from the distribution of characters. It does not compute a correct answer; it compares candidates and picks one, so it can be wrong.

By our own measurement, across 148 cases in 12 languages the first candidate is correct 84.5% of the time and the correct answer is among three candidates 96.6% of the time. These figures apply to that sample and are not a guarantee for every input. Accuracy drops sharply in particular when:

  • the input is shorter than about ten characters;
  • characters have already been replaced by ? or � — the original information is gone, and no tool can restore it;
  • invisible characters were lost while passing through a messenger or terminal.

For anything important, keep the original safe before you use this tool. The operator accepts no responsibility for loss arising after a recovered result has been written over an original.
